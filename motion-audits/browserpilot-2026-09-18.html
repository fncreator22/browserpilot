Overall Assessment

BrowserPilot demonstrates deep architectural craft and impressive visual presence, but its motion layer suffers from asymmetrical transitions (modals and search states animate into the DOM but vanish with 0ms pops on exit) combined with pervasive AI-slop tells like infinite pulsing indicators, reflow-heavy height animations, and an absence of useReducedMotion() accessibility hooks.

Critical Jakub Krehel Motion Gap

Zero Exit Animations on Modals & Conditional Views

What Over 150+ conditional renders across the app use animate-in fade-in on mount, but disappear instantly from the DOM on unmount without an exit transition.

Why it matters Asymmetrical motion breaks user spatial continuity. A modal that smoothly expands into view but vanishes abruptly on ESC reads as a crash or rendering glitch.

Recommended motion

Wrap conditional overlays and views in <AnimatePresence mode="wait"> with an asymmetric curve: 220ms enter (opacity + scale 0.96 -> 1) and a faster 140ms exit (opacity + translateY 6px).

Zero useReducedMotion() in Framer Motion Components

What While globals.css contains a fallback for CSS animations, zero components in the repository use useReducedMotion(). Framer Motion spring physics and scroll listeners run uninhibited.

Why it matters Users with vestibular disorders who enable "Reduce Motion" in their operating system will still be subjected to scroll-linked 3D rotations in ScrollComparisonSection and JavaScript-driven RAF loops.
